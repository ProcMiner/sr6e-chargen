Release Notes

What's shipped, in the order it shipped — newest first. This is a hobby project built for a small group of friends, so "releases" just means "pushed to the live site," not a formal version scheme.

August 17, 2026

  • New
    Preview Character Sheet
    A "Preview Character Sheet" button sits next to "Download Character Sheet" in both the player builder and an NPC's Full Build - generates the same PDF but opens it in a new browser tab's built-in viewer instead of saving a file, so you can check it looks right without cluttering your downloads.
  • Fix
    Body/Strength/etc. cyberware and bioware bonuses now actually show up
    Attribute-boosting augmentation (Muscle Augmentation, Muscle Replacement, Bone Lacing, and the like) was silently invisible everywhere except the specific stats an earlier fix already covered (Reaction, Initiative Dice, Armor) - the chargen Summary's attribute list, unarmed Attack Rating, Defense Rating, Astral Combat, Matrix Initiative, and LivePlay's Combat/Astral/Matrix/Advancement panels all read your raw natural attribute only, so a character could never actually see their Strength or Body climb past their metatype's natural maximum no matter how much cyberware they installed. Every one of those now reads the augmented (natural + gear bonus) value, shown alongside the natural score wherever they differ (e.g. "9 (8 natural)"). Confirmed correct for both build systems (Priority and Life Path) and all five metatypes' attribute ranges against the core rulebook's table. Also caught and fixed a separate, narrower bug found in the same pass: Bone Lacing (Plastic)'s own +1 Body/+1 Armor was listed in its stat block but never wired to actually apply, unlike its Aluminum/Titanium siblings.
  • New
    Convert Karma to nuyen
    The Gear section now has a "Convert Karma to nuyen" control (core rulebook p.66, "Spend Customization Karma") - 2,000¥ per Karma point, or 5,000¥ if you've taken the In Debt quality. This was a real rule with zero way to actually do it in the app before now; the In Debt quality's own description already referenced it. Chargen only (hidden once a character moves into Live Play), and both the Gear picker and the Summary sheet show the extra nuyen and spent Karma alongside their usual totals.
  • Fix
    Knowledge/Language and specialization suggestion fields
    Two testers reported being unable to type anything into a Life Path module's Knowledge/Language field - the text box just sat there. The one thing that field had that a normal text input doesn't is a native browser autocomplete (an <input list> wired to a <datalist> of suggested topics), which is exactly the kind of thing that behaves inconsistently across browsers. Replaced it, along with the two other spots using the same pattern (skill specialization suggestions in both the Priority builder and LivePlay's Advancement panel), with plain text inputs plus a row of clickable suggestion chips underneath - same suggestions, no native popup involved.
  • Fix
    Exceptional (Attribute) quality now actually raises the attribute max
    Purchasable for 12 Karma with zero mechanical effect - the quality's own text ("its maximum ... increases by 1") was never wired to the attribute cap anywhere, so buying it (and picking, say, Strength) changed nothing. Now folded into the same attribute-range calculation the metatype and metavariant caps already go through, so the attribute editor's max, Advancement's post-creation cap, and every other consumer of a character's attribute ranges all pick it up automatically.

August 16, 2026

  • Fix
    Cyberjacks now actually boost Matrix stats
    All six Cyberjack ratings were storing their Data Processing/Firewall and VR Matrix Init Dice bonus under a display-only label that no code ever read - a decker's cyberjack silently contributed nothing to their computed Attack Rating, Defense Rating, or VR Initiative. Fixed; every decker with a cyberjack should see their Matrix stats jump to the correct (higher) numbers.
  • New
    Drain Resistance
    A new "Drain Resistance" section at the top of LivePlay's Astral Plane panel, computing the dice pool that reduces drain damage: Willpower + Tradition Attribute for spellcasting/conjuring/enchanting, or Body + Willpower for activating an adept power - the book uses a genuinely different formula for adepts, so a Mystic Adept sees both numbers side by side.
  • New
    Collapsing Now NPCs: Runner Groups
    28 more "Import from book" stat blocks, drawn from Collapsing Now's generic archetypes across six organizations: Freedom Network (7), GreenWar (3), Grey Cell (6), Ordo Maximus (5), Stark/Theissen/Van der Mer (3), and The Yakuza (4). Named plot-specific individuals (Anna Moller, the Sea Dragon's inner circle, the Shadow Chapters' leadership, etc.) were intentionally left out - same "reusable stat block, not a story character" boundary as the roster's Sample Contacts and Paragons exclusions. NPC roster is now 163 entries across 5 sourcebooks.
  • New
    Firing Squad NPCs: Fighting Forces
    35 more "Import from book" stat blocks - Lone Star, Knight Errant, five smaller corp security providers, Humanis Policlub paramilitary, and five street gangs (Ancients, Halloweeners, Cutters, First Nations, Smokers Club), straight from Firing Squad's "Fighting Forces" chapter. NPC roster is now 135 entries across 4 sourcebooks.
  • New
    Full Build for NPCs
    Every NPC on the roster can now opt into "Build Full Character" - the exact same Priority/Life Path builder a player character gets, with the same computed Attack Rating, Defense Rating, and every other derived stat. For the quick mook, the roster's existing lightweight free-text stat block still works exactly as before; this is for the rare NPC - a BBEG, a named rival - that deserves a complete build. Also generates a real character sheet PDF for them.
  • New
    Character Generator User Guide
    A new text walkthrough covering both build systems (Priority and Life Path) start to finish, the steps every character shares (Contacts, Qualities, Lifestyle, PACKs, Gear, saving), moving a finished character into In Play, and how GM's Bar works - session creation, the party dashboard, the NPC roster, and giving an NPC a Full Build. Linked as its own line under the main nav bar. Written by walking both build flows directly in the app rather than from memory, so the steps match what's actually on screen.
  • New
    Reputation & Heat
    A new "Reputation & Heat" panel in LivePlay for every character - both are GM-judgment calls rather than dice-rolled formulas, so it's a simple +/- adjuster next to the book's own reference tables (Reputation Changes, Heat Effects with your current tier highlighted, Heat Roll Modifiers, and the three ways to lower Heat). Reputation 10+ or -10 or lower shows its Edge-threshold effect live. GM's Bar's session view now shows each connected character's Reputation/Heat at a glance, plus the party's highest Heat with its effect - the book's own rule for group effects. Run Compensation (the 5-Karma / 5,000¥ award guidance) is included as reference text in the same panel, not a new tracked stat, since Karma/Nuyen awards already exist.
  • New
    Mundane Combat: Attack Rating, Defense Rating, and a Combat reference panel
    Two real computed numbers, finally: unarmed Attack Rating (Strength + Reaction) and Defense Rating (Body + worn armor + augmentation bonuses), both shown in the chargen Summary and a new "Combat" panel in LivePlay. Worn armor correctly sums your single best owned Clothes/Armor item (base suits aren't cumulative with each other per the book) plus every Helmet/Shield you own (those do stack). The same LivePlay panel also carries reference tables for the 5-step Combat Process (including the Attack/Defense Rating Edge-distribution rule and the Initiative tie-break order), the combat Edge-spend table, range bands, firing-mode modifiers, elemental damage types, and Off-Hand/Knockdown/Multiple Attacks/Grapple/Cover/Barriers.

August 15, 2026

  • New

    Closes out the gear-catalog rollout for good. Four PACKs in the picker - Dirty Tricks (electrochromic paint, oil slick sprayer, road strip ejector, spike strips, thermal smoke projector, gas dispersal system, shredder tires, spoof kit), Gun Ports, Rigger's Baby, and Combat Biker - each expanding into real modification lines (improved handling/acceleration/stability, top speed increase, nitro boost, elemental hardening, rigger cocoon, a shielded smuggling compartment) sourced directly from Double Clutch.
  • New
    Complete Character PACKs
    16 full-nuyen pre-built character kits in the PACKs picker - Shadowrunner Starter, Dirt Poor, Full Magician, Close Combat Adept, Face, Gunslinger Adept, Cybered Covert Operative, Decker, Full Conversion Cyborg, Street Samurai, Vat Job Bioware-Augmented Combat Specialist, Weapons Specialist, Max Hardware Decker, Transport Rigger, Drone Rigger, and the standalone Augmented for Firearms PACK. Most nest the Starter PACK inside themselves and grant a month of lifestyle automatically; augmentations and foci are itemized so Essence and Karma-bonding costs come out right (Close Combat Adept's Weapon Focus lands on the book's exact 9 Karma to bond).
  • New
    Contacts, in both build systems
    Add contacts and raise their Connection/Loyalty ratings right in the builder. Priority System uses the book's Charisma × 6 point pool (no rating above Charisma). Life Path draws points from your chosen life modules plus Coming of Age instead - Charisma doesn't limit it at all, matching the book's explicit callout that these are genuinely different rules. Life Path contacts can also be pushed further with customization Karma, capped at Charisma.
  • New
    Knowledge & Language skills, in both build systems
    Priority System gets a picker for the first time: a free pool equal to your Logic, spent on knowledge topics or language levels (Basic/Specialist/Expert). Life Path's per-module knowledge picks now support the same language leveling - pick "Sperethiel" from two different modules and it becomes one Sperethiel entry at a higher level instead of two separate ones. Every character also gets one free Native language, tracked separately from either budget.
  • Fix
    Knowledge/Language free allotment was documented wrong
    The Rules Coverage page said Priority System's free Knowledge/Language count was a "Logic+Intuition" budget - the book actually says Logic alone (Logic+Intuition is Memory, an unrelated test). Corrected; the new picker uses the right number.
  • New
    Priority System power-level variants: Street-level and Prime Runner
    A toggle above Priority Assignment. Street-level reads every category's table values one row worse than the letter you picked - Attributes B reads as C, and so on, floored at row E. Prime Runner keeps your priorities as chosen but doubles customization Karma from 50 to 100. Straight from the book's "Different Levels of Play" sidebar.
  • Fix
    Rules Coverage's "Out of scope" badge relabeled
    Renamed to "Roadmap Item" and recolored blue (was gray) - covers Metasapients, Quality Paths, and the NPC roster entries.
  • New
    Four more spirit types: Plant, Guardian, Guidance, Task
    From Street Wyrd's "A Congress of Spirits" - summon them the same way as the six core-rulebook types, with their own Force-scaled stats (including a couple of oddball formulas transcribed exactly as printed, like Guardian Spirits' doubled Defense Rating and Task Spirits' unusually generous Condition Monitor). Six new powers added to the glossary to support them: Divining, Magical Guard, Shadow Cloak, Silence, Skill, and Skill Specialization.
  • New
    Astral Reputation tracking
    A new -10 to 10 score in the Conjuring panel showing how spirits as a community regard you, with the book's mechanical thresholds called out automatically (e.g. AsRep 8-9 means spirits you summon arrive at +1 Force) and a reference table of what raises or lowers it.
  • New
    The Astral Plane: Assensing, Astral Projection, Astral Combat, Astral Tracking, Mana Barriers
    A new panel in LivePlay for any character with a Magic rating - the Assensing hits-to-information table, Astral Projection's rules and computed time limit before Essence loss (Magic × 2 hours), Astral Combat's Attack Rating/Defense Rating/Initiative computed from a new Tradition Attribute choice in the builder (Logic or Charisma), the Astral Tracking modifiers table, and the Mana Barriers table. Also shown on the character sheet summary once you've picked a Tradition Attribute.
  • New
    Decker Persona Configuration
    A new builder panel for anyone carrying a cyberdeck or commlink/cyberjack. Attack/Sleaze/Data Processing/Firewall aren't fixed to one device - they're freely assembled from the pool of printed numbers across all your Matrix-capable gear, exactly like the book's Erika MCD-6 + Transys Avalon example. Pick your split and the app computes Attack Rating, Defense Rating, and Matrix Initiative for AR, VR cold-sim, and VR hot-sim.
  • New
    The Matrix: reference panel for deckers and technomancers
    A new panel in LivePlay alongside The Astral Plane - Matrix Initiative and Attack/Defense Rating (pulled from your Decker Persona or Living Persona), device Matrix Condition Monitors, Overwatch Score & Convergence with the Matrix Edge Actions table, Dumpshock/Link-Locking/Noise, Matrix Perception & access levels, and full glossaries of all 33 Matrix Actions and all 20 Programs. Hosts and IC stay out of scope - GM-facing hacking-encounter content, not character-build data.
